The CultureBlocs meetup.

A friendly London evening for people at every stage — tech-curious, practitioners, technologists, founders — to talk about what they're building, share problems, and explore what technology can do for the cultural sector. Whether you work in music, performance, theatre, visual arts, museums, nightlife, or digital products: newcomers welcome.

The shape of an evening

  • 6:30 — Arrivals
    Grab a drink, meet people, say hello.
  • Welcome to CultureBlocs
    A short intro to what this is, why we're bringing people together, and how newcomers can get involved.
  • Shares
    Informal shows of ideas, products, creative projects, and problems people would like to explore with the group.
  • Open conversation
    How is technology changing creative work? Where can data genuinely help artists, audiences and cultural organisations? What tools and spaces are missing?
  • Newcomer-friendly networking
    Smaller groups: ask questions, find collaborators, meet people doing interesting things.
  • Afterwards
    More drinks and informal chat, if you'd like.

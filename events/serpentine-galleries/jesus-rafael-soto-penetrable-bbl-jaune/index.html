About

A walk-through sculpture in Kensington Gardens beside Serpentine South: some 4,000 hanging yellow PVC tubes on a 10-metre steel frame, finished by the movement and touch of the people passing through it. Soto (1923–2005) began the Pénétrable series in 1967; this is a 2023 edition of a 1999 work. Free.

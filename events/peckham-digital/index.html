Peckham Digital 2026.

The fifth Peckham Digital, a festival of creative computing in south-east London: two exhibitions, hands-on workshops, a Processing Community Day, talks, office hours and nights of live audio-visual performance, across Copeland Park and The Hub, 22–25 October, day by day. Every event here is an open calendar record in the @cultureblocs.com repository — so if you went, you can say so in a bead of your own that points straight at it.

About

Jeremy Osztreicher introduces modular synthesis as a way to build small generative sound systems, patching oscillators, clocks, envelopes, filters and randomness in Noodlerack, a free modular synth that runs in the browser. Beginners welcome; bring a laptop and headphones. £16.80, concession £10.70 (incl. VAT).

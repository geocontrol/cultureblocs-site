Events / Games Transformed

Games Transformed 2026.

A festival of games and radical politics in Bethnal Green — this year’s theme, Here to Stay, Here to Play, takes racial and migrant justice as one fight. Panels, workshops, performance, a youth and families programme, the motherlode afterparty and 36 games to play, across Proposition Studios, Pelican House and Three Colts Tavern, 30 October – 1 November, day by day. This listing is a demonstration compiled by CultureBlocs from the festival's Ticket Tailor page; Games Transformed doesn't publish it. If you went, you can say so in your own account, and your record will point at the event.

The festival hasn’t published session times, rooms or speakers yet, so the panels and workshops show Time tbc under the day they’re on. They’ll be filled in here when the timetable appears.

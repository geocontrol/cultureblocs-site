An example, not the official listing

An example compiled by CultureBlocs from Frieze's and other organisers' public announcements and published from @cultureblocs.com, to show how I went could work. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Frieze or any other organiser listed. Only a handful of records point at it. Times change, so check the venue before you travel.

Events are records

Each event is a community.lexicon.calendar.event record, the shared calendar format used across the network. This page reads them live, so a correction appears here without a site update.

Your visit is yours

Went to something? Write a visit bead in your own repository and reference the event by its at:// address — open Details on any event to copy it — or just press I went, which writes it for you. The memory stays with you; the event is just what it points at.

If Frieze published this

Frieze, or any organiser listed here, could publish its events as records in its own account and put an I went button on each event page. Anyone who pressed it would keep the record in their own account, pointing at your event, with no CultureBlocs page in between.

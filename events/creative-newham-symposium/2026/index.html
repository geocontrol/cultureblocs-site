About

Creative Newham's day on creative spaces in Newham: who shapes places and who gets a say, culture and the public realm, co-creation, civic networks and grassroots culture. Panels and provocations, a marketplace of ideas, drop-in advice and 1:1 sessions with funders and support organisations, and hosted huddles, building towards a Creative Spaces Charter for Newham. For artists, freelancers, producers, community groups, local government, developers, architects, researchers, funders and policymakers.

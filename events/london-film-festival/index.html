How this listing works

One film, one record

Each film is a community.lexicon.calendar.event record published from @cultureblocs.com — one per film, not per screening. It runs from the first screening to the last and lists them all. A short shares its programme’s screenings.

What you saw is yours

Seen something? Press I saw and it writes a screening bead to your own account, pointing at the film. Or write one yourself — open Details on any film to copy its at:// address. Saw it somewhere else, or later? The bead still points at the same film.

Facts, not blurbs

Titles, credits, strands and screening times come from the BFI’s festival pages. The synopses are the BFI’s own, so they stay there: every film links to its BFI page, which is also where tickets are.

Independent

An unofficial listing compiled by CultureBlocs. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the BFI. Screenings change and sell out — check the BFI before you travel.

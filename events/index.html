Run one of these programmes?

You can publish your programme yourself, in your own account, using the shared calendar format. You don't need our permission or our tools to do it. If you do, we'll switch the listing to read from your account and work with you so the records people have already made still point to the right place.

If something here is wrong, or you'd rather it wasn't listed, get in touch and we'll correct or remove it.

What these listings are testing

We're not adding listings by hand any more. Each one so far was built to answer a question:

  1. Will organisers publish their own programme in an open format, if it costs them little beyond what they already do?
  2. Will people say they went when it takes one tap from a listing or a printed card?
  3. What does a programme look like when you can see what went together, across events no single organiser runs?

New listings will come from organisers who publish their own programme, or from a box office or calendar feed they already run.

How these listings work

The events are community.lexicon.calendar.event records, the shared calendar format used across the network, not a CultureBlocs one. For these demonstrations they're published from @cultureblocs.com. Each page reads the records live, so a correction appears without a site update.

When you press I went, the record is written to your account and points at the event. Pages here find those records through an open index of the network and read each one from its author's account. Delete yours and it disappears from the page.

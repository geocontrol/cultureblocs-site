An open data layer for cultural life.

CultureBlocs is a shared, open way to record creative and cultural activity as small pieces of data. Making work, putting on a show, watching, reading, visiting: anyone can publish it, and anyone can build on it.

Making

create · share · perform · present · curate · host

Experiencing

watch · listen · view · read · visit · take part

An exhibition and the people who saw it. A film and everyone who watched it. CultureBlocs records both, in the same format.

Every record is a bead

A bead is one small record: who did what, with which work, where and when. Each one is tiny. Published together, by artists, venues and audiences, they show cultural activity that no single platform, venue or box office can see today.

What people do with it

An artist

Publishes a CreativeID, then a bead for each new work. Her own website, a gallery's site and an open-call portal all read the same records, so she updates once.

A venue

Publishes its season as event records. Visitors who choose to share an "I went" bead point back to the event, so the venue can count who came without collecting anything itself.

A listener

Lets a scrobbler turn plays into listen beads. They stay on her own machine until she picks which to share.

A festival or researcher

Reads published beads across a city or a weekend to see participation beyond ticket sales, using data nobody had to hand over to a platform.

Say who you are, once

Artists and cultural practitioners can publish a CreativeID: a short public profile of who you are and what you do, made in the Persona Studio. It's yours, in your words, and readable by any app that uses CultureBlocs.

There is no CultureBlocs platform

CultureBlocs publishes three things. There is nothing to sign up to, and anyone can build something with the data.

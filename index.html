Cultural memory, strung by hand.

CultureBlocs is a set of open schemas and small tools for keeping a record of your cultural life — the exhibitions, gigs, films, books and encounters that make it up. Moments are minted as beads, told into strands, and kept in a spine you run yourself. Publishing is a deliberate act, never a default.

Local-first

Beads are minted on your devices and live in your own spine. Nothing needs the network to work; a gallery basement is a fine place to remember something.

Own your data

The record store runs on your machine. Every schema is open, every export is yours, and the tools are small enough to read.

Privacy by default

Everything starts private. Sharing is a promotion you perform on purpose — per bead, per strand — with locations fuzzed and devices stripped on the way out.

How a moment travels

totem press ──> Studio (date, name, tell) ──> Spine (your record store) │ AR bookmark ───────────────────────────────────────┤ scrobbled listening ──────────────────────────────>│ ▼ the day's string · strands │ deliberate publish ▼ your website · (soon) your ATProto repo

The same schemas describe a bead whether it lives on a device, in your spine, or published to the open web — privacy is a property of where a record lives, not of its shape. That is what makes local-first and federation two ends of one thread rather than two systems.

What's a bead?

A moment, kept

A bead marks one cultural moment: a visit, a talk, a film. String them together and they become a day's strand — a diary of culture you actually lived.

Yours, not ours

It lives in your account's repository, beside your posts. CultureBlocs keeps nothing: no server, no copy. Delete it any time.

How this works — and how it should

Events are records

Each exhibition, talk and night is a community.lexicon.calendar.event record. This page reads them live from the repository, so a correction shows here without a site deploy.

the listing →

Your visit is yours

Press I went and a visit bead is written to your own Atmosphere account (Bluesky, Blacksky…) pointing at the event. The memory stays with you; the event is just what it points at.

write a bead →

Beads that know where they are

Alongside each record the listing carries the venue’s address and coordinates, the exhibition a talk belongs to, the people involved and the kind of bead to suggest — what a totem or Pocket Totem needs to mint a bead with its context already filled in.

Better from the gallery

This is a demonstration made by CultureBlocs. The right publisher is the gallery: post these records from its own account, link “I went” from its own pages, and every visitor’s bead points at the source. Nothing here needs a CultureBlocs server.

Reference tools.

Small apps that read and write the CultureBlocs format. None of them hold your records: they work on your own account or on your own machine. Use them, copy them, or build your own. The format is what matters; any of these can be replaced.

In your browser, nothing to install

All of these sign in with your own AT Protocol account and write only to it.

I went ready

Press I went on an event in a listing, write a line, and sign in. A record pointing at that event is written to your own account, with an optional Bluesky post.

find an event →

My beads ready

Every bead in your account, from any device: search them, correct them, delete them or add new ones. It changes only the fields you touch and won't overwrite a bead edited elsewhere. Installs as an app on your phone.

open My beads →

My creative persona ready

Publish your CreativeID: the name you're credited under, what you do and where you're based. Anything the form doesn't show is left as it is.

make yours →

Easel ready

Claim your works: a record per work with images, alt text, dates and credits, published to your account. It tracks what has changed since you last published.

open Easel → · claim a work →

Publish an event ready

A form that writes one event, in the shared calendar format, to your organisation's account. Pair it with a venue profile.

publish an event → · venue profile →

The wall ready

One person's published strands, newest first, with a link per strand. Reads their account directly.

the wall →

Catalogue ready

An artist's published works as a portfolio page, in a grid, index or feature layout. Point it at a handle.

the catalogue →

Strand embed ready

A small web component that shows published strands on any site, live from an account or from an exported file.

embed it →

Run your own

For people who want their own store or index. Each runs in one container.

The String ready

A small self-hosted record store: one SQLite file on your own disk. Checks every record against the format, keeps a change history, and publishes when you choose. (Formerly “the Spine”; old spine:// addresses still work.)

source →

Loom in development

A local-first desk for writing and keeping your own records, working with or without a String. Runs in a browser with no build step. The roadmap is in the repository.
